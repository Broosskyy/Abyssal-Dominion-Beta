# Sea life, events and menu expansion after data 245

- Organized captain menu with grouped destinations, a persistent Back button, parent-menu history and Escape/back navigation. Compact ammunition/items pickers retain their contextual placement. Action labels wrap and headers reserve distinct space for back/title/close.
- Three saved quickbar pages, three configurable consumable slots each. Slot art, live stock, toggle status and cooldowns come from the actual inventory. Configure via the gear or captain menu. Himmelsbrand launches a real extra explosive projectile (valid enemy ship/tower, 650 m, 35 s cooldown). Lebensamulett heals 25% base current max hull; Segen der Tiefe heals and applies the existing timed crystal blessing. New items are stocked in the harbor; invalid activations consume nothing. Cooldowns now persist across app restart.
- Tiamat plus Kraken, Nerissa sea serpent and Karapax armored crab on normal seas. Each has distinct generated transparent art and real monster combat/loot/respawn. Harpoons remain required. Existing lower-map neutral-aggression rules remain. Movement and a restrained idle float are implemented; these three assets are sprites, not rigged 3D creatures. Their data-driven presentation keys allow later model replacement.
- Original floating glimmer salvage replaces small loot boxes. Halloween pumpkins and Columbus explorer finds form an independent collectible layer. Seven active finds per standard sea respawn after 120 sea seconds. Each grants its separate saved event token, 30 gold and one relevant special item. Event selection/off is saved. These are explicitly selectable beta previews, without calendar scheduling or final economic balancing.
- Halloween Night Crown and Columbus Explorer Crown are skins 12/13 on the exact existing Black Crown GLB. Geometry, original test model and Elite 01–10 are preserved. Both have distinct generated albedos and real model-rendered previews, available in Events and the shipyard. New schema 7 preserves event skins; schemas 1–6 still migrate and do not reinterpret retired skin 12 as an event unlock.
- Unified screen-space reward and damage readouts maintain consistent typography across sea zoom. Powder shots carry actual bonus metadata (+40% on the existing eligible ammunition), with orange muzzle feedback and a persistent stock/toggle indicator. Plated hits carry the actual reduction metadata (−35%), with blue readouts and a persistent armor aura/status indicator. Feedback does not draw over menus or the top HUD.
